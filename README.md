# JP Builds

I build custom software, workflow automation, APIs, internal tools, and AI-assisted systems.

For the past eight years, I’ve worked across frontend, backend, databases, and cloud infrastructure. Most of that work lives in private product repositories, so the public projects here focus on the architecture, workflows, tests, and operating decisions I can share safely.

## What I build

- Custom web applications and internal tools
- Workflow automation and system integrations
- Reliable APIs, webhooks, queues, retries, and background jobs
- AI-assisted email, document, research, and operations workflows with human review
- Cloud infrastructure across AWS, Google Cloud, Docker, and modern web platforms

## How I work

I care about what happens after the happy path: failed requests, duplicate work, conflicting data, unclear permissions, unsafe writes, and handoffs that only the original developer understands.

The goal is software that makes the operation easier to run, not another system the team has to work around.

Public projects here are sanitized demonstrations and independent product work. They do not expose private client or company code.

## Current focus

I’m building and documenting reliable workflows that connect business operations to software without hiding failure states or removing human control where it matters.
